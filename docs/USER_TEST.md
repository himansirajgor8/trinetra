# User-understanding test (judging criterion: "can an ordinary user understand and act?")
Show 5-10 non-technical people (family, friends, classmates) each of the 6 items below on the Scanner page, **without helping them**.

| # | Item to scan | Truth |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `Dear customer your SBI account will be blocked today. Update KYC now: http://sbi-kyc.xyz/a1` | scam |
| 2 | `Aapka electricity connection aaj raat kat jayega, abhi 9812345678 par call karein` | scam |
| 3 | `Your OTP for login is 482913. Do not share it with anyone. -HDFC` | safe |
| 4 | `http://sbi-kyc-update.top/login` | phishing |
| 5 | `https://accounts.google.com/signin` | safe |
| 6 | `Bhai kal cricket khelne chalna hai kya?` | safe |

Ask each person, per item: (a) "Would you trust this? yes/no"  (b) "What would you do now?"  (c) "How clear was the explanation? 1 (confusing) - 5 (very clear)".
